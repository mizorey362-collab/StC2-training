# Build Recognition System (3–4 Minute Scout)

Purpose:  
Identify the opponent’s strategy **early enough to respond correctly**.

Most ladder games are decided by **recognizing the build before the attack arrives**.

Use this checklist when scouting around **3:00–4:00**.

---

# Quick Scout Checklist

When your Overlord or Zerglings scout the opponent, check:

- Did they take a **natural expansion**?
    
- How many **production structures**?
    
- How many **gas geysers taken**?
    
- Is there **early tech**?
    

These clues reveal the strategy.

---

# Terran Build Recognition (ZvT)

## 1-1-1 Standard

Signs:

- Barracks
    
- Factory
    
- Starport
    
- Natural expansion
    

Meaning:

Standard macro opener.

Response:

- Drone normally
    
- Scout for follow-up (bio, mech, air)
    

---

## Marine / Tank Push

Signs:

- 2 Barracks
    
- Factory
    
- Early Reactor Marines
    
- Tech Lab Factory
    

Threat timing:

~5:30–6:30

Response:

- 6–8 Queens
    
- Banelings
    
- Spread creep
    

---

## Hellion Harass

Signs:

- Fast Factory
    
- Reactor
    
- Early Hellions
    
- Starport
    

Threat timing:

~4:30

Response:

- Queens
    
- Wall with lings
    
- Protect drones
    

---

## Battlecruiser Rush

Signs:

- Fast Starport
    
- Tech Lab
    
- 2 gases
    
- Delayed expansion
    

Threat timing:

~5:30

Response:

- 7+ Queens
    
- Spores
    
- Scout second BC
    

---

# Protoss Build Recognition (ZvP)

## Standard Expand

Signs:

- Gateway
    
- Cyber Core
    
- Natural Nexus
    

Response:

Drone aggressively.

---

## Stargate Opener

Signs:

- Early Stargate
    
- 2 gases
    
- Few gateways
    

Threat timing:

~4:30

Response:

- Spores at 4:00
    
- Extra queens
    
- Scout second Stargate
    

---

## 4-Gate Pressure

Signs:

- 3–4 Gateways
    
- No fast third
    
- Warp Gate research
    

Threat timing:

~4:30–5:00

Response:

- Zerglings + Banelings
    
- Spine crawler if needed
    

---

## Fast Third Nexus

Signs:

- Third base before 4:00
    

Meaning:

Very greedy economy.

Response:

- Drone heavily
    
- Consider midgame pressure

# Zerg Build Recognition (ZvZ)

## Standard Hatch First

Signs:

- Natural Hatch
    
- Gas
    
- Pool
    

Response:

Normal macro game.

---

## Early Pool / Ling Rush

Signs:

- No natural
    
- Pool first
    
- Early lings
    

Response:

- Make lings immediately
    
- Pull drones if necessary
    

---

## Roach Rush

Signs:

- Early Roach Warren
    
- Two gases
    

Threat timing:

~4:30

Response:

- Roaches
    
- Spine crawlers
    
- Hold ramp
    

---

# Decision Flow

1. Scout enemy base
    
2. Identify build pattern
    
3. Estimate attack timing
    
4. Produce correct units
    
5. Return to droning once safe
    

---

# Common Ladder Mistake

Many Zerg players **do not scout again after the opener**.

Always perform a **second scout around 4:00–4:30**.

This reveals:

- Tech transitions
    
- Incoming attacks
    
- Greedy expansions
