## Core Rule

Drone until you see a reason not to.

---

## 0:00–2:00 Opening

Standard opener:

13 Overlord  
16 Hatch  
18 Gas  
17 Pool

Scout enemy expansion.

If enemy **does not expand → prepare defense**.

---

## 2:00–3:30 Expansion

Goals:

- Start queens
    
- Zergling speed
    
- Third hatch
    

If opponent expands → drone aggressively.

---

## 3:30–5:00 Threat Window

Scout with:

- Overlord sacrifice
    
- Zerglings
    

If attack coming → build units  
If safe → drone to 55+

---

## 5:00–6:30 Midgame Setup

Goals:

- Lair
    
- 4th base
    
- Drone to 66
    

---

## 6:30–8:00 Power Economy

Goals:

- 75–80 drones
    
- Hive start
    
- Army production begins
