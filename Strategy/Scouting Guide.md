# Zerg Scouting Guide

A practical scouting system for **StarCraft II** that tells you **what to scout, when to scout, and what the information means**.

The goal is to answer the most important Zerg question:

**“Should I drone or make army?”**

---

# Core Zerg Scouting Principle

Zerg relies on **information to maximize economy**.

Rule:

**Drone until scouting tells you to stop.**

---

# Scouting Timeline

|Time|Method|What To Look For|
|---|---|---|
|1:30–2:00|Overlord|Enemy natural expansion|
|3:30–4:00|Ling scout / Overlord|Tech structures|
|4:30–5:00|Overlord sacrifice|Army size / tech|
|6:00+|Ling / Overseer|Tech transitions|

Each scout answers **different questions**.

---

# 1:30–2:00 Scout (Opening Information)

Your first Overlord should reach the enemy natural around this time.

Check:

- Is the **natural expansion taken?**
    

## If YES

Opponent is playing standard.

Action:

- Continue droning
    
- Take your third base
    

## If NO

Potential early aggression.

Prepare:

- Extra queens
    
- Zerglings
    
- Defensive positioning
    

---

# 3:30–4:00 Scout (Tech Identification)

Use:

- Zerglings
    
- Overlord near main ramp
    

Check:

- Number of **production buildings**
    
- **Gas geysers**
    
- Tech structures
    

## Key Clues

|Sign|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Many production buildings|Early attack|
|Few buildings|Fast tech|
|Extra gas|Advanced tech incoming|

---

# 4:30–5:00 Scout (Threat Confirmation)

Perform an **Overlord sacrifice** into the enemy main.

Check:

- Tech structures
    
- Army composition
    
- Expansion count
    

## Important Discoveries

|What You See|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Large army|Attack soon|
|Tech structures|Transition build|
|Third base|Greedy economy|

---

# 6:00+ Scout (Midgame Planning)

Use:

- Overseer
    
- Zerglings on map
    
- Creep vision
    

Look for:

- New tech structures
    
- Army movement
    
- Additional bases
    

---

# Overlord Positioning Guide

Good Overlord placement gives **early warning**.

## Positions

|Location|Purpose|
|---|---|
|Enemy natural|Confirm expansion|
|Outside enemy main|Scout tech|
|Attack paths|Spot armies|
|Behind enemy base|Overlord sacrifice|

---

# Zergling Map Control

Once you have Zerglings, spread them across the map.

Place them:

- Outside enemy natural
    
- At watchtower
    
- On common attack routes
    

Purpose:

**Detect attacks early.**

---

# Signs of Incoming Attack

Watch for these clues:

|Sign|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Army leaving base|Attack soon|
|No third base|All-in possible|
|Many production buildings|Aggression build|

Response:

- Stop droning
    
- Produce units
    
- Prepare defense
    

---

# Signs You Can Drone Greedily

If you scout these signs:

|Sign|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Early third base|Greedy opponent|
|Few units|Safe economy|
|Defensive structures|Passive play|

Action:

- Drone heavily
    
- Expand
    
- Tech up
    

---

# Scouting Tools

Zerg has many scouting methods.

|Unit|Purpose|
|---|---|
|Overlord|Early information|
|Zergling|Map control|
|Overseer|Main base scouting|
|Creep tumors|Vision control|

Using **all of them together** gives the best information.

---

# Common Scouting Mistakes

Avoid these frequent errors.

## Not Scouting After Opening

Many players stop scouting after the first Overlord.

Always scout again around **4 minutes**.

---

## Ignoring Map Vision

Without Zerglings or creep on the map, attacks arrive **too late to react**.

---

## Not Interpreting Information

Scouting only helps if you **react correctly**.

Always ask:

- Is an attack coming?
    
- Can I drone?
    
- Should I prepare defenses?
    

---

# Scouting Checklist During Games

Ask yourself every minute:

- Did I scout recently?
    
- Do I know my opponent’s tech?
    
- Do I see their army movement?
    
- Am I safe to drone?
    

---

# Ideal Zerg Information Loop

Scout → Interpret → React → Drone

Repeat this cycle throughout the game.

The better your scouting becomes, the easier your decisions become.
