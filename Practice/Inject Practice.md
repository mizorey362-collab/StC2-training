# Inject Practice

> Goal: inject every ~29 seconds. Benchmark: [[Zerg Macro Benchmarks]]

---

## Rating Guide

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 5 | Perfect inject cycles throughout — no idle queens |
| 4 | Minor delays only, <10s gaps occasionally |
| 3 | Some missed injects, gaps up to 30s |
| 2 | Frequent delays or forgotten bases |
| 1 | Very inconsistent — queens often idle |

**Target: 4+ in ladder games, 5 in drills.**

---

## Drill Methods

### Drill 1 — Pure Inject (vs Easy AI)
1. Play macro-only vs Easy AI — no army moves
2. Focus entirely on maintaining inject at all hatches
3. Measure inject rate in replay afterward
4. **Target: 90%+ inject rate, 0 queens idle > 15s**

### Drill 2 — Back-Space Method
1. Hotkey all queens to `2`
2. Press `2` → `V` (inject) → click hatch
3. Press `BackSpace` → camera returns to last position
4. Repeat across all bases
5. Practice until you can inject 3 hatches in < 5 seconds

### Drill 3 — Inject Under Pressure (vs Medium AI)
1. Play a real game vs Medium AI with aggression on
2. When attacked: **inject first, then micro army**
3. Accept worse army control — reward yourself for not missing injects
4. Goal: inject rate should not drop more than 10% vs pure drill
