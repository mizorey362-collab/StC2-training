# Creep Spread Practice

> Creep gives vision, speed, and map control. Units move ~30% faster on creep.
> Reference: [[Zerg Macro Benchmarks]] · [[Macro Timeline (0-10 Minutes)]]

---

## Benchmarks

| Benchmark | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| First tumor placed | ~3:30 |
| Second tumor wave | ~4:30 |
| Creep reaching mid-map | ~7:00 |
| Creep rating in games | 4+/5 |

---

## Tumor Placement Rules

1. **Always place at maximum creep edge** — not in the middle of existing creep
2. **Spread in multiple directions** — toward enemy, toward 3rd base, toward watchtower
3. **Natural queen walks forward** — she should be moving toward 3rd after early tumors placed
4. **Inject first, creep second** — sync into your inject cycle loop

---

## Drill Methods

### Drill 1 — Pure Creep Focus
1. Play vs Easy AI, macro only
2. At 3:30: natural queen places first tumor toward enemy
3. Activate new tumors every 15s
4. Goal: **How far does creep reach by 7:00? By 10:00?**

### Drill 2 — Inject + Creep Sync
1. Every time you complete an inject loop: immediately check natural queen
2. Move queen + place or activate 1 tumor
3. Return to army/drones
4. Goal: full inject loop + creep check in under 8 seconds
