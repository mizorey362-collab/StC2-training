## 3-Hatchery Inject Schedule

Once you have 3 hatcheries:
- Main Queen → inject + creep when energy allows
- Natural Queen → inject + push creep chain forward
- 3rd Queen → inject 3rd hatch, then walk to 4th

Inject cycle time: **~29 seconds** (25 energy at 1 energy/~0.95s)

See [[Inject Practice]] for drills.
